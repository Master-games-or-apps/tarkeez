# Tarkeez (تركيز)

**Focus. Execute. Achieve.**

The ultimate task and goal management platform built for Arab teams. Organize work, connect everyday tasks to strategic objectives, and keep everyone focused on what actually matters — all in one place.

## Overview

Tarkeez is an all-in-one task management and OKR (Objectives and Key Results) platform designed for collaborative teams. It comes with full bilingual support for Arabic and English, so your whole team can work comfortably in the language they prefer.

The goal is simple: stop the fragmentation. Bring tasks, goals, and collaboration into one flexible, intuitive workspace instead of juggling multiple tools.

## Key Features

- **Flexible Tasks**  
  Interactive boards, custom lists, and timelines so your team can organize work the way that fits them best.

- **OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)**  
  Link daily tasks directly to strategic goals and track progress and completion rates in real time.

- **Seamless Team Collaboration**  
  Shared workspaces built for teams from day one, with full support for Arabic layout and typography.

- **Bilingual Interface**  
  A polished experience that works seamlessly in both Arabic and English.
